# Personal Projects

### 1) Thesis. "Volatility: A Path-Dependent Approach"

Residual analysis of Guyon's path-dependent volatility model on a VX/ES dataset, extension of the
model to the intraday timescale and infinite-OU representation, forecasting comparison against a CatBoost microstructure
model (0.57 directional accuracy).

[Paper](thesis/thesis_Ilia_Demskii.pdf) · [Presentation](thesis/thesis_presentation_demskii.pdf)

### 2) Asymmetric Random Walk and Catalan Numbers

A novel formula for the distribution of the maximum of an asymmetric random walk connected to
Catalan numbers.

[Paper](asymmetric_random_walk/course_paper_year_3_demskii.pdf)

### 3) Portfolio Construction. Random Matrix Theory & Bayesian Shrinkage

Asset-selection model for a CHF 574k portfolio. Two approaches: Marchenko–Pastur denoising with a constrained
minimum-variance frontier, then a Bayesian mean-shrinkage model with a CVaR objective and turnover
control.

[Presentation](portfolio_optimization/presentation_portfolio_demskii.pdf)
